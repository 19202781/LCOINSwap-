How to Buy LCOIN

1

Connect your wallet – Click "Connect Motor" and choose Phantom, Solflare, or Backpack.

2

Choose what to swap – Select SOL, USDT, USDC, PUMP, JUP, or paste the mint address of another token.

3

Enter the amount – Type how much you want to spend. The calculator shows how many LCOIN you'll receive.

4

Confirm in your wallet – Review the network fees in your wallet app and sign the transaction.
